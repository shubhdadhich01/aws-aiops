# Contributing

Thanks for wanting to make this better. This repo is part of the CareerByteCode
learning ecosystem, so every change needs to stay on-brand and pedagogically
sharp. This guide is short on purpose.

---

## Before you open a PR

1. **Read [`playbook.md`](./playbook.md)** — it defines the CBC voice, structure and
   copy blocks that every CBC repo shares.
2. **Open an issue first for anything larger than a typo.** A quick "here's what I
   want to change, and why" saves you writing time and us review time.
3. **Match the day-folder pattern.** Every day has the same shape (README,
   trainer-notes, interview-qa, teardown-checklist, diagrams/, lab/). If your
   change breaks the pattern, it needs a very good reason.

---

## What we happily accept

- Bug fixes in Terraform, Python, or lab steps
- Clarifications in the READMEs where the wording tripped you up
- Additional interview Q&A (please match the existing tone)
- Better diagrams (Mermaid preferred; keep them in `diagrams/`)
- Translations of a day's README — file it as `README.<lang>.md` in the same folder
- Cost-savings tips that don't compromise the learning outcome

## What we'll usually push back on

- New days or major curriculum changes (open an issue and let's talk)
- Renaming folders or files (breaks links across the repo)
- Adding new dependencies to Python labs without a strong reason
- Changing the finding contract (severity weights, score floor, IDs) — that
  contract is intentionally locked

---

## Voice & tone

CBC speaks like a friendly, senior mentor who's been through it. Direct. Warm.
Practical. Never salesy, never academic. See `playbook.md` §10 for examples.

**A quick self-check before you submit:**

- Would a working engineer read this and say "yes, that's the real trade-off"?
- Is every URL on `careerbytecode.in` (never `.com`)?
- Are any personal names in the copy? (They shouldn't be, except in the
  Founding Team block and the Sangeetha CTA — those are approved.)

---

## Attribution

Every accepted contribution gets a shout-out on our channels and, if you
consent, a mention in the day's README. Add your name to
`CONTRIBUTORS.md` (create it if it doesn't exist) as part of your PR.

---
